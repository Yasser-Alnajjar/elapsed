QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How does billing work?

Elapsed subscriptions are billed flat monthly in USD. There are no credit cards required to start. Invoices are issued directly to your organization on a net-14 settlement schedule. When your trial ends or when you initiate a paid plan, your direct invoice is generated in your Billing tab. You can cancel at any time—cancellations always take effect at the end of the current billing period, and monitoring never pauses.

What happens if we exceed integration or policy limits?

Nothing is ever switched off. Going over your plan's integration or SLA policy count simply shows an informational warning in your Settings. Ingestion, handoff calculations, alerts, and historical proof chains continue running uninterrupted.

How do plan upgrades and downgrades take effect?

Upgrades (Starter → Team) apply immediately, and the prorated difference for the remaining days in your cycle is added to your next direct invoice. Downgrades (Team → Starter) remain active until the end of your prepaid period. If your current team has more seats in use than the target plan allows (e.g. 7 seats used vs. Starter's 5 limit), you must remove members before scheduling the downgrade.

Who in our company has permission to modify billing?

Only the designated Organization Owner can subscribe, change plans, or schedule cancellation. Organization Members have read-only access to view current plan entitlements and download invoice receipts.
