Direct Answers

Frequently asked questions

Clear, deterministic billing policies without unexpected seat expansions.

More answers in the docs →
How does billing work? expand_more
Billing is charged as a flat monthly rate per organization in USD. We do not charge per-seat fees or metering surcharges based on ticket ingestion volume. Your organization receives an itemized invoice each month on net-14 payment terms.
What happens if we exceed integration or policy limits? expand_more
Elapsed operates under our operational continuity guarantee. If your team temporarily exceeds configured integration or policy thresholds, telemetry, case correlation, alerts, and historical data never halt. You will receive an informational notice on your account status strip with options to align your plan.
Do policies imported from Zendesk count toward the limit? expand_more
No. Policies and schedules synchronized read-only from your Zendesk instance do not count toward your Elapsed active policy allowance. Only bespoke composite cross-tool policies authored inside Elapsed count against policy limits.
How do upgrades and downgrades take effect? expand_more
Plan upgrades take effect immediately upon confirmation, and the prorated price difference for the remainder of your billing cycle is added to your next invoice. Plan downgrades remain scheduled and take effect precisely at the end of the current billing cycle.
Who in our company can change billing? expand_more
Only users designated as Organization Owners possess permission to modify payment methods, upgrade tiers, or request enterprise terms. Standard Organization Members are granted read-only visibility into active plan limits and historical invoices.
What happens when the trial ends? expand_more
When your 14-day full access trial concludes without selecting a paid plan, live webhook ingestion and notifications pause. Your historical stage logs and metrology audit data remain securely preserved for 90 days, enabling instant resumption when an owner enters billing details.
