ABOUT ELAPSED

Support and engineering, working off the same clock.

A customer request rarely stays in one system. It starts as a support ticket, gets escalated to engineering, waits on a release, and comes back to the customer. The SLA clock keeps running the whole time. We built Elapsed to make that whole path visible in one place, before anything breaches.

01 / DIAGNOSIS

The problem we kept running into

Helpdesk tools know about the support side of a case. Engineering trackers know about the engineering side. Neither knows about the other, so the moment a ticket is handed to engineering its SLA effectively goes dark: tracked in a spreadsheet, a Slack thread, or nowhere at all.

By the time someone notices a commitment is close to breaching, it's often because the customer mentioned it first. We think that's backwards.

“One honest elapsed-time number per commitment, across every system the work touched.”

03 / DEFINITION

What Elapsed is — and isn't

Elapsed is

Capabilities
  • check One clock per commitment that survives the handoff
  • check A timeline of where the time went, by stage
  • check A warning while there's still time to act
  • check A record you can open in a QBR to explain a number

Elapsed is not

Non-Goals
  • remove A workflow or ticketing tool
  • remove A verdict on which team is at fault
  • remove An AI that guesses links or predicts outcomes
  • remove A service-credit or financial calculator
